
# Privacy Policy for Visual Spectrum

**Effective Date:** March 11, 2026

Visual Spectrum respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not collect, and how locally saved app data is used.

## Information We Collect

Visual Spectrum does **not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information** or personally identifiable data from users.

The app does not require account creation and does not gather information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location data
- Contacts
- Photos
- Payment information
- Usage analytics
- Advertising identifiers

## Local Device Data

Visual Spectrum may save certain app preferences and reading-related information **locally on your device only** in order to support app functionality.

This may include:

- App settings or preferences

This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

Visual Spectrum does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services to collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

Visual Spectrum does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users.

## Data Sharing

Because Visual Spectrum does not collect personal data, no personal data is sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

## Security

Since Visual Spectrum does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no user personal information stored on external servers by the developer.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by posting the revised version with a new effective date.
